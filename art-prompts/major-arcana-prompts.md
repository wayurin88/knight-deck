# Major Arcana — Image Prompts (22 cards)

How to use: copy the BASE PROMPT, then paste one "SPECIFIC ARCANA" block (below) at the very top of it.
Replace [ROMAN NUMERAL] and [ARCANA NAME] with the values in the block.
Recommended size: 2:3 portrait, e.g. 1024x1536 or larger.

Consistency tips
- Generate all 22 with the same model, same style reference (use the first good card as a style reference / --sref) and a fixed seed where possible.
- AI generators often garble lettering. Safer workflow: add the line "leave the top numeral panel and the bottom title panel completely blank" to the prompt, then add the numeral and the name as text in the game.
- Palette hint by element (matches the game): Fire = ruby red, amber, antique gold. Water = sapphire, cobalt, teal. Air = pale sky blue, ivory, silver-gold. Earth = emerald, amber, deep brown-gold.

## BASE PROMPT

Create a premium fantasy Major Arcana tarot card designed as a collectible card game illustration, combining Japanese anime fantasy character design with the visual language of Gothic cathedral stained glass windows.

OVERALL STYLE
Japanese fantasy anime illustration fused with intricate medieval Gothic stained glass artwork. The entire artwork should look as if it has been constructed from real pieces of colored cathedral glass, separated by elegant dark lead lines and framed with aged gilded metal. Highly detailed, sophisticated, dramatic, mystical, elegant, sacred, and slightly mysterious. Premium fantasy trading-card artwork, suitable for a high-end collectible card game.

COMPOSITION
Strict vertical tarot card composition, approximately 2:3 aspect ratio. Strong symmetrical and highly organized composition. Ornate Gothic stained-glass border surrounding the entire card. Elaborate gold filigree and medieval decorative frame. Decorative celestial motifs in the four corners. Large symbolic halo or geometric stained-glass motif behind the main character. Main character prominently centered. Strong visual hierarchy. Rich foreground, middle ground, and background. Cathedral architecture and fantasy scenery integrated naturally into the stained-glass composition. Decorative title panel at the bottom. Roman numeral at the top. Every element should feel intentionally designed as part of one coherent tarot card.

MAIN CHARACTER
A beautiful Japanese anime-style fantasy character representing the meaning and archetype of the specific Major Arcana. Elegant anime proportions, expressive eyes, detailed hair, sophisticated fantasy clothing, layered robes, ornamental accessories, jewelry, magical artifacts, and symbolic objects. The character should feel like a legendary fantasy-game character rather than a generic anime character. Pose, facial expression, costume, weapon, magical object, surrounding creatures, and environment should all communicate the specific symbolism of the selected Major Arcana.

STAINED GLASS TREATMENT
Everything must visually integrate with authentic stained glass. Use translucent colored glass, irregular hand-cut glass pieces, visible dark lead came lines, subtle glass texture, jewel-tone colors, luminous highlights passing through glass, slight imperfections in the glass, rich cobalt blue, ruby red, emerald green, sapphire, amethyst, amber, ivory, antique gold. The lighting should appear as though sunlight is shining through an enormous cathedral stained-glass window. Important: do not make it look like ordinary digital anime artwork with a stained-glass filter applied afterward. The character, clothing, architecture, background, magical effects, and decorative elements should all be constructed visually as stained glass.

SYMBOLISM
Use classical Tarot symbolism as the conceptual foundation while reinterpreting it through a Japanese fantasy world. Include symbolic objects and motifs appropriate to the specific Major Arcana. Symbols should be visually integrated rather than randomly scattered. Use sacred geometry, celestial symbols, flowers, animals, weapons, magical artifacts, architectural motifs, and elemental symbolism where appropriate.

COLOR & LIGHT
Deep jewel-tone palette with luminous stained-glass illumination. Strong contrast between dark lead lines and brilliant translucent colored glass. Dramatic cathedral lighting with glowing highlights around the character. The image should feel luminous from within rather than simply brightly colored.

CARD FRAME
Elaborate collectible-card frame with antique gold metal, Gothic arches, ornamental filigree, small stars, celestial symbols, geometric patterns, decorative corner ornaments, subtle engraved details. At the top: [ROMAN NUMERAL]. At the bottom: [ARCANA NAME]. Typography elegant, classical, readable, integrated naturally into the card design.

QUALITY
Extremely detailed fantasy illustration, intricate linework, sophisticated character design, beautiful facial rendering, complex stained-glass construction, premium collectible card artwork, cinematic lighting, polished composition, museum-quality decorative artwork.

No modern objects. No photographic realism. No 3D-rendered appearance. No generic playing-card design. No excessive UI elements. No random text. No watermark. No logo.

The final image should look like a legendary Japanese fantasy tarot card displayed as a magnificent Gothic cathedral stained-glass window.
