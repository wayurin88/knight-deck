# Minor Arcana — Image Prompts (56 cards)

Same visual language as the Major Arcana set (Japanese anime fantasy x Gothic cathedral stained glass), but each suit has its own frame accent and palette so the four suits are recognisable at a glance.

## How to use
1. Copy the MINOR BASE PROMPT.
2. Paste the SUIT BLOCK of the card's suit at the top.
3. Paste the CARD BLOCK (one scene line) under it.
4. Fill the two text fields exactly as written in the card block: `TOP TEXT` and `BOTTOM TEXT`.

Tips
- Attach 2-3 of your finished Major cards as a style reference (e.g. --sref in Midjourney, or an image reference), so the Minors match the Majors.
- When filling the text fields, type the words without square brackets. The first Major (The Fool) came out as "[0 · THE FOOL]" because the template brackets leaked into the image.
- If the generator keeps garbling letters, add: "leave the top panel and the bottom title panel completely blank" and add the text in the game instead.
- Generate one whole suit in a row (A to King) with the same seed or style reference so the suit stays consistent.
- Size: 2:3 portrait, 1024x1536 or larger. Export as .jpg and name the file with the id listed in each card block (art/README.md explains the naming).

## MINOR BASE PROMPT

Create a premium fantasy Minor Arcana tarot card designed as a collectible card game illustration, in the same style as the Major Arcana set: Japanese anime fantasy character design fused with the visual language of Gothic cathedral stained glass windows.

OVERALL STYLE
Japanese fantasy anime illustration constructed entirely from translucent cathedral stained glass: irregular hand-cut glass pieces, dark lead came lines, subtle glass texture, luminous highlights as if sunlight shines through an enormous cathedral window, slight imperfections in the glass. Everything (characters, clothing, architecture, background, magical effects) must be built as stained glass, not an ordinary digital illustration with a filter. Highly detailed, sophisticated, sacred and slightly mysterious. Premium collectible card game artwork.

COMPOSITION
Strict vertical tarot card composition, 2:3 aspect ratio. One clear scene told by the main characters and objects, with a strong focal point, rich foreground, middle ground and background, and cathedral architecture or fantasy scenery integrated into the glass. A large symbolic stained-glass rose window or geometric halo behind the scene. The suit symbol is repeated in the scene exactly as many times as the card's number says, clearly countable, integrated into the composition (not scattered).

CHARACTERS
Beautiful Japanese anime-style fantasy characters with elegant proportions, expressive eyes, detailed hair, layered fantasy clothing and ornamental accessories. They must feel like legendary fantasy-game characters, never generic anime characters. Poses, expressions and costumes communicate the meaning of the card.

CARD FRAME
Elaborate collectible-card frame in antique gold metal with Gothic arches, ornamental filigree, small stars, celestial symbols, geometric patterns and decorative corner ornaments, with a subtle accent color taken from the suit. A decorative top panel and a decorative bottom title panel. Typography is elegant, classical and readable. Write exactly the TOP TEXT in the top panel and exactly the BOTTOM TEXT in the bottom panel, with no brackets, no extra words.

COLOR & LIGHT
Deep jewel-tone palette with luminous stained-glass illumination; strong contrast between dark lead lines and brilliant translucent colored glass; dramatic cathedral lighting; the image should feel luminous from within.

QUALITY
Extremely detailed fantasy illustration, intricate linework, beautiful facial rendering, complex stained-glass construction, polished composition, museum-quality decorative artwork.

No modern objects. No photographic realism. No 3D-rendered appearance. No generic playing-card design. No UI elements. No random text. No watermark. No logo.
